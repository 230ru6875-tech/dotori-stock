# AdSense approval checklist

## Before submitting

- Replace `dotoristock.com` with the final custom domain if you use a custom domain.
- Add the real AdSense publisher ID to the site head:
  - `<meta name="google-adsense-account" content="ca-pub-0000000000000000">`
- Copy `ads.txt.example` to `ads.txt` and replace the publisher ID with the real value from AdSense.
- Confirm `https://your-domain/ads.txt` returns HTTP 200.
- Register the domain in Google Search Console.
- Submit `sitemap.xml`.
- Confirm the following pages are indexed or at least crawlable:
  - `/`
  - `/reports/`
  - `/about.html`
  - `/privacy.html`
  - `/terms.html`
  - `/disclaimer.html`
  - `/seo.html`

## Content quality

- Keep the dashboard working with current public data.
- Keep at least 10 stock report pages available under `/reports/`.
- Avoid empty cards, placeholder text, and broken links.
- Keep investment wording informational, not directive.
- Do not promise profit or imply guaranteed prediction accuracy.

## After submitting

- Do not click your own ads.
- Do not ask visitors to click ads.
- Keep ads visually separate from menus, buttons, and report content.
- Review AdSense policy center if Google reports an issue.
